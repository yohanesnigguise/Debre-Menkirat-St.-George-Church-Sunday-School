About Our Fellowship

Fre Haimanot Sunday School

ፍሬ ሃይማኖት (Fruit of Faith)

Preserving Sacred Traditions for Every Generation

Established under the spiritual guidance and benediction of Debre Menkirat St. George Church, Fre Haimanot Sunday School (ፍሬ ሃይማኖት ሰንበት ት/ቤት) serves as a spiritual haven for youth, children, and families in Dallas, Texas.

Our motto is to cultivate authentic Orthodox Christian spiritual fruit through biblical wisdom, Saint Yared's sacred music, charitable works, and deep fellowship within the body of Christ.

Introduction to Divine Liturgy (ሥርዓተ ቅዳሴ)

Understanding the Orthodox Mass structure for students

Beginner Level

1. Preparation (ሠርዓተ ቅዳሴ)

Preparation of the sacred bread (Qurban) and wine in the Bethlehem, prayers of thanksgiving, and cleansing of the sanctuary.

2. Liturgy of the Catechumens (ቅዳሴ ትምህርት)

Readings from the Pauline Epistles, Catholic Epistles, Acts of the Apostles, and the Holy Gospel followed by the Nicene Creed.

3. The Anaphora (ፍሬ ቅዳሴ)

The central Eucharistic prayer of thanksgiving, consecration of Holy Mysteries, and Holy Communion.
